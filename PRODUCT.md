# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The meeting facilitator and participants working in small groups during a Department discussion of the proposed General Academic Regulations.

## Product Purpose

Help the facilitator direct the meeting and help each group record its observations in the proposal's Ficha 8 online. Success means groups can contribute separately, the facilitator can follow their progress, and the department can review the recorded observations and supporting files.

## Positioning

Group-specific links connect a shared online Ficha 8 for each group to a facilitator panel, with Google Sheets and Drive as the record and file store.

## Operating Context

The 90-minute meeting uses a four-option semáforo: Mantener, Revisar, Modificar, and Incorporar. Groups discuss assigned regulatory areas and record observations; the facilitator reviews progress and the groups prioritize their most important observations in conversation afterward.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The web app is served by Google Apps Script and uses the supplied Google spreadsheet and Google Drive.
- The facilitator creates groups during the meeting. Each group has its own link and shared ficha.
- Group links work without Google sign-in. A separate facilitator code protects the panel that displays all group fichas and progress.
- The facilitator panel includes the meeting opening, suggested timing, prioritization prompts, discussion axes, and optional cases from the proposal.
- Ficha 8 retains its six fields: Artículo, Semáforo, ¿Qué plantea?, Problema/tensión, Principio en juego, and Propuesta.
- Groups can upload documents, images, and phone-recorded audio up to 25 MB per file. Files can be attached to an observation or saved as general group material.
- The facilitator can close and reopen each group's ficha. Priority selection remains a discussion activity, not a web form field.

## Evidence on Hand

- The attached “Revisión propuesta Reglamento General Académico” describes the meeting purpose, agenda, Ficha 8, and discussion axes.
- The user supplied a Google Sheets workbook ID for the backend.

## Product Principles

- Keep each group's work distinct while making overall progress visible to the facilitator.
- Preserve the proposal's six-field Ficha 8 and four-option semáforo.
- Make group participation possible without a Google account.
- Keep supporting files with the observation or group conversation they document.
- Give the facilitator control over access to the combined panel and when group work is editable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The app is browser-based for use by groups during an in-person meeting. No specific accessibility standard or participant access needs have been established.
